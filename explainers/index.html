F5 TMM · embedded userspace eBPF · a design proposal

A verified, in-data-plane programmability surface for TMM.

TMM runs its own stack and bypasses the kernel — which makes it powerful and nearly impossible to instrument from outside. Embed a small, verified eBPF VM inside it and you get a third programmability surface: observe internal state, and enforce surgical CVE mitigations — loadable in hours, and proven — by a verifier — unable to hang or corrupt the data plane.
