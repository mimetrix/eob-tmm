The pipeline derives a predicate, emits a constrained one-liner, compiles it, and puts it through three mechanical oracles — verifier, exploit-replay, and a false-positive check — before a human ever signs. One scenario passes clean, one fails an oracle and self-corrects, one is declared un-shieldable. This mirrors the working tmmtrace prototype.
